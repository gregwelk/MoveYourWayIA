1Two paths to becoming an Ambassador

Ambassadors build on their Champion experience by reaching organizations and the wider community. Choose the path that fits you, or do both.

Organizational liaison

Partner with a school, worksite, congregation, library or senior center you know well, and help it become a Move Your Way partner.

GivePulse coordinator

Become an editor or administrator for your county’s GivePulse page and help coordinate local resources, events and partners.

Supported every step

Ambassador Training covers both paths, including how to manage and use GivePulse. Our CCPH team is here to help.

Not a Champion yet? Start with the Champion page and earn your Champion badge first.

2Five priority settings

Ambassadors partner with schools, worksites, congregations, libraries and senior centers to make movement visible and routine, using a customized Organizational Outreach Guide for each setting. The guides are shared after Ambassador Training.

Tap a circle to see why the setting matters and a few first ideas.

3Take the keys: lead GivePulse in your county

GivePulse is the Pulse of the Movement: it’s where the action happens. Our team can support you, but an important part of the Ambassador role is taking the keys and running local programming more directly. Ambassador Training shows you how to manage and use these five features:

Resources

Post guides, pledge materials and promotion ideas for your county.

Events

Post walks, workshops and trainings, with sign-ups and reminders.

Wall

Share wins, photos and ideas, and cheer on local efforts.

Subgroups

Connect your county page with schools, worksites and other partners.

People

Invite and message Movers, Champions and fellow Ambassadors.

Built to grow over time. This year, we’re building county pages with Ambassadors helping to coordinate them. Over time, partners like a school district, worksite or senior center can have their own GivePulse page that affiliates with the county page.
