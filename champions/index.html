1You’re ready to be a Champion

A Move Your Way Champion helps family, coworkers, neighbors and community groups find practical, enjoyable ways to move more. You don’t need a fitness background or a title.

Lead by example

Share your own pledge and your move in your everyday spaces, in a way that fits you.

Pick a strategy

Each strategy below comes with a ready-to-use message template and simple steps.

Log your impact

Add what you did as an Impact on GivePulse to earn your Champion badge.

Not a Mover yet? Start with the Mover page: make your pledge, share it and join on GivePulse.

2Choose your strategy

Here are 10 ways to help others connect with Move Your Way. Mix and match: there’s no minimum and no wrong way to do it. Every person you help connect counts.

Tap a circle to see how it works and open the full strategy handout.

3Make it count on GivePulse

GivePulse is the Pulse of the Movement: it’s where the action happens. Every strategy points people there, and it’s where your work as a Champion counts.

  • Invite people to Join so they can find events, tips and other Movers in their county.
  • Add an Impact with the blue Add Impact button: the strategy you used, who you reached and what happened.
  • Claim your Champion badge on the Badges tab, and keep going toward Ambassador.

Haven’t joined yet? Select Join on the GivePulse page to become a member first.
