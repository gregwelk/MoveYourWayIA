About the Campaign

MoveYourWayIA is an Iowa campaign based on the national Move Your Way® model. Its message is simple: lots of things count as physical activity, and it all adds up. We want moving more to feel doable, welcoming and fun for every Iowan, at every ability and in every corner of the state.

The plan is built from the ground up. Lasting change happens in counties, in the coalitions, partnerships and local leaders that grow there. Counties choose to join. None are required to, and every county is welcome.

Iowa Physical Activity PlanThe Iowa Physical Activity Plan is a grassroots effort focused on building engagement and capacity at the county level. It is designed to help counties strengthen local leadership, build partnerships, and connect with peers across the state. The goal is not to ask counties to adopt a one-size-fits-all model. The goal is to help county leaders start where they are, build on local strengths, and gradually create the teams and relationships needed to promote active living over time. The MoveYourWayIA campaign is powered by GivePulse which provides counties with a powerful tool to promote community engagement, manage volunteers, plan events and even raise funds. As county partners build capacity, we will build connections and partnerships between counties and across the state. Together we can move Iowa.
Healthy Iowans · Active Living WorkgroupThe Active Living Workgroup of Healthy Iowans helps to coordinate and spread the MoveYourWayIA campaign as part of state level goals to increase awareness, provide access to evidence-based programs and to make connections to support programming. The Workgroup has committed to advance the grassroots approach of the Iowa Physical Activity Plan. If you are interested in the process and plans for this effort please contact the co-chairs of the Active Living Workgroup: Greg Welk (gwelk@iastate.edu) and Allie Burkey (aburkey@iastate.edu).
Community Connections for Public HealthThe CCPH group is a community outreach initiative of the ISU Translational Research Network (U-TuRN). The CCPH team oversees the management of the campus GivePulse network at ISU and supports the activities of a linked student organization called the Community Engagement Club. The CCPH team has had long-standing partnerships with local agencies but gradually accepted roles that extend beyond local programming by coordinating partnerships, supporting service learning, facilitating statewide outreach, and helping connect campus priorities with community-based public health work. Informal ‘Academic Health Department’ affiliations were established with some counties but the county-level engagements established here help to formalize these partnerships to ensure mutual benefits to both student leaders and county partners. Undergraduate students contribute to CCPH activities and the MoveYourWayIA initiative in a variety of ways: course-based service learning, independent studies, honors projects, internships and volunteering. AmeriCorps members and Graduate students contribute to project management, community building and program evaluation. The program is student powered and this is key to sustainability.
Getting Started

Start where you are

You don't need a gym, special gear or a big block of time. Movement can happen all through the day, and even 5 minutes has real health benefits: a better mood, sharper focus, less stress and better sleep. Keep four Move Your Way messages in mind:

Some is better than none

Any activity beats sitting still.

Lots of things count

Housework, gardening, walking the dog, taking the stairs.

It all adds up

A few minutes at a time, all through the day.

Find what works for you

So it's easier to stick with.

Here is how much activity the Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans recommend.

Adults
150min / week

of moderate activity like brisk walking, plus muscle-strengthening on 2 days a week.

Kids & teens 6–17
60min / day

of moderate-to-vigorous activity, including activities that build muscles and bones.

Older adults
150min / week

plus muscle-strengthening on 2 days, and activities that improve balance.

Your first week

  1. Pick something you enjoyWalking the dog, dancing in the kitchen, biking to the store. If you like it, you'll keep doing it.
  2. Start smallEven 5 or 10 minutes at a time counts toward your weekly total. Build up from there.
  3. Make a planUse the Move Your Way activity planner to map out your week.
  4. Make it publicMake a pledge so your county shows up on the map, then challenge a friend, coworker or neighbor to do the same.

Join Spring into Summer

Build habits for active living through the annual Spring Into Summer initiative. The FREE, 6 week, fully online program is fun and easy to do.

The online program follows the established ‘Walk with Ease’ intervention model but is customized to provide guided support on building healthy habits. Whether you're just starting or looking to stay consistent, this flexible program makes it easy to build or reinforce healthy habits for a more active lifestyle. Participants receive a weekly email with a video lesson and access to an online portal with resources, podcasts and self-monitoring tools.

The attached flyer has all the information you need to sign up. You can scan the QR code or register directly by clicking this link or by going to this website: https://startwalkwithease.org/Welcome/iaworksite.

Be sure to select your county when you register as we want to reach all 99 counties. Programming runs from Jan 1–March 1st. We encourage you to coordinate with family, friends or coworkers to help you start together and to stay motivated!

Badge Up

Level up the movement

Join the movement. Help it grow. Lead it forward. Everyone starts with a pledge, and that's a complete way to take part. When you're ready, you can help others get moving and earn a Champion or Ambassador badge. Students and non-students can earn both.

Level 1 · Join the movement

Mover

“I made my move, shared it, and connected with the movement.”

  • Make a pledge: “This week, I will get active by…”
  • Share it with at least one person. Private counts.
  • Join GivePulse and log your pledge with your county.

Recognition: a welcome from the team. No training, quotas or hours.

Level 2 · Help it grow

Champion

“I help people in my county or campus connect with Move Your Way.”

  • Join your county's GivePulse page, or a campus group.
  • Invite at least five people to pledge, or have one real conversation with a local organization.
  • Log your outreach with a short reflection.

Recognition: verified Champion badge, single teal ring.

Level 3 · Lead it forward

Ambassador

“I organize opportunities and help others put active living into action.”

  • Hold Champion status and complete a short orientation.
  • Plan and run an activity or promotion with at least one other helper or partner.
  • Record what happened and what comes next.

Recognition: verified Ambassador badge, double teal and gold ring.

Two tracks, one movement

The badge names where you contributed, not who you are. Earn an Iowa badge for work in a county and an ISU badge for work on campus. If you do separate work in both places, you can earn both.

Iowa track

Help a county grow its movement: invite neighbors, connect local organizations and support county events with AmeriCorps members and local partners.

ISU track

Build an active campus with a residence hall, class, student organization, department or work group. Campus energy feeds the county network.

ISU students: three ways to be a Champion

Pathway A

Reach your home county

Use your own connections to bring friends, family and local groups to your home county's page.

Pathway B

Adopt a county

Get matched with an Iowa county that wants help and support its local partners, wherever you're from.

Pathway C

Promote active living with students, faculty and staff through tables, classes and campus groups.
