Your body is meant to move

You don’t need a gym, special gear or a big block of time. Movement can happen all through the day, and even 5 minutes has real health benefits: a better mood, sharper focus, less stress and better sleep.

1Keep these points in mind

Physical activity is a reward, not a chore. Find what works for you, commit to an active lifestyle and keep moving.

Some is better than none

Every bit of movement counts, even 5 minutes at a time.

The best activity is the one you’ll do

Any activity beats sitting still. Pick something you enjoy.

Lots of things count

Playing, housework, gardening, walking the dog and taking the stairs all add up.
