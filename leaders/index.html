Leaders: bring the movement to your whole county

Leaders represent their county in the Iowa Physical Activity Plan. They connect with other Leaders across the state, learn what works in each sector, and bring proven strategies home.

1From Ambassador to Leader

Many Iowans become Champions. Some go further as Ambassadors and create change in their own school, worksite or congregation. A few step up as Leaders, often people who already lead in their field, like a chamber of commerce director, a school superintendent or a health system leader.

Represent your county

Serve as your county’s voice for one sector of the Iowa Physical Activity Plan.

Connect statewide

Join the statewide Leader network, organized by sector, to share what works with Leaders from other counties.

Bring strategies home

Help local Ambassadors and partners put evidence-based strategies to work in your county.

2Explore the 10 sectors

The National Physical Activity Plan, developed by the Physical Activity Alliance, organizes evidence-based strategies into 10 sectors. Each one is a place where Iowans can help their communities move more.

Tap a circle to learn about a sector and open its handout.

3GivePulse: the Pulse of the Movement

This website tells the story. GivePulse is where the action happens. It’s where Leaders, Ambassadors, Champions and Movers connect, find events and log their impact.

  • Go to the MoveYourWay Iowa page on GivePulse.
  • Select Join in the upper right to become a member.
  • Find your county under Subgroups, and log your work as an Impact to earn your badges.
